{{further|2025 Rolex Shanghai Masters}}

Qualifier [[Valentin Vacherot]] defeated his first cousin [[Arthur Rinderknech]] in the final, 4–6, 6–3, 6–3 to win the singles tennis title at the [[2025 Rolex Shanghai Masters|2025 Shanghai Masters]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.espn.com/tennis/story/_/id/46570136/valentin-vacherot-beats-cousin-arthur-rinderknech-win-shanghai-masters|title=Valentin Vacherot beats cousin Arthur Rinderknech to win Shanghai Masters|publisher=ESPN|accessdate=12 October 2025}}</ref> It was his first [[ATP Tour]] title, and he defeated five seeded opponents consecutively en route. Vacherot became the first Monégasque player to win a tour singles title, the first [[Seed (sports)|unseeded]] champion at the [[Shanghai Masters (tennis)|Shanghai Masters]], and the lowest-ranked [[ATP Masters 1000 tournaments|ATP Masters 1000]] champion (ranked No. 204). Vacherot was the third qualifier (after [[Roberto Carretero]] and [[Albert Portas]] at the [[1996 Panasonic German Open – Singles|1996]] and [[2001 Hamburg Masters – Singles|2001 Hamburg Masters]], respectively) to win a Masters 1000 title,<ref>{{cite news|url= https://matchtenis.com/masters-1000-shanghai-2025-los-registros-de-valentin-vacherot-tras-consagrarse-campeon/|title= Masters 1000 Shanghái 2025: los registros de Valentin Vacherot tras consagrarse campeón|language=es|date=12 October 2025|work=matchtenis.com|access-date=12 October 2025}}</ref> and the fifth player overall to win an ATP 1000 as their first tour-level title.<ref>{{cite news|url= https://firstsportz.com/arthur-rinderknech-cousin-valentin-vacherot-tears-shanghai-masters/|title= (Video) Emotional Arthur Rinderknech Brings Cousin Valentin Vacherot to Tears After Shanghai Masters Final Clash |date=12 October 2025|work=firstsportz.com|access-date=12 October 2025}}</ref>

For the first time in the tournament's history, two unseeded players contested the final, and this was only the third all-unseeded ATP 1000 final (after the 1996 Hamburg Masters and the [[2003 BNP Paribas Masters – Singles|2003 Paris Masters]]).<ref>{{cite news|url= https://www.atptour.com/en/news/rinderknech-vacherot-shanghai-2025-final-sunday |title= Vacherot completes Shanghai fairytale, defeats cousin Rinderknech for maiden title |date=12 October 2025|work=ATP Tour|access-date=8 October 2025}}</ref> As first cousins, Vacherot and Rinderknech were the first pair of family members to contest an ATP Tour final since brothers [[John McEnroe|John]] and [[Patrick McEnroe]] at the 1991 Chicago Open.{{citation needed|date=October 2025}}

[[Jannik Sinner]] was the defending champion,<ref>{{cite news|url= https://www.atptour.com/en/news/djokovic-sinner-shanghai-2024-final|title= Sinner in seventh heaven with dominant Shanghai final win vs. Djokovic|date=12 October 2024|work=ATP Tour|access-date=8 October 2025}}</ref> but retired in the third round against [[Tallon Griekspoor]].<ref>{{cite news|last=Nelson|first=Craig|url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/tennis/articles/c4gj3vgvz14o|title=Sinner retires but Djokovic through in Shanghai|work=BBC Sport|date=5 October 2025|accessdate=6 October 2025}}</ref>

At {{ayd|1987|5|22|2025|10|9}} old, [[Novak Djokovic]] reached a record 80th ATP 1000 semifinal and extended his record as the oldest semifinalist (since the format's establishment in 1990).<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.atptour.com/en/news/djokovic-bergs-shanghai-2025-qf |title= Stayin' alive in Shanghai! Djokovic tops Bergs for M1000 history|website=ATPTour|date=9 October 2025|accessdate=10 October 2025}}</ref> The second-round match between Djokovic ({{ayd|1987|5|22|2025|10|3}}) and [[Marin Čilić]] ({{ayd|1988|9|28|2025|10|3}}) was the oldest Masters 1000 singles match in terms of combined ages (75 years and 139 days) since the format began in 1990.<ref>{{cite news|last=Coombe|first=Jerome|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/djokovic-cilic-shanghai-2025-friday|title=Djokovic brushes off Cilic in Shanghai opener|work=ATP Tour|date=3 October 2025|accessdate=3 October 2025}}</ref>
