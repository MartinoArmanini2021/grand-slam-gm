{{further|2026 National Bank Open}}

Defending champion [[Ben Shelton]]<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.atptour.com/en/news/shelton-khachanov-toronto-final-2025 |title= Shelton claims ATP Masters 1000 breakthrough in Toronto! |website=ATPTour|date=8 August 2025 }}</ref> defeated [[Brandon Nakashima]] in the final, 6–3, 7–6<sup>(7–4)</sup> to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2026 National Bank Open|2026 Canadian Open]]. He did not lose a set en route to his second [[ATP Masters 1000 tournaments|Masters 1000]] title and seventh [[ATP Tour]] title overall. Shelton was the first player to successfully defend the men's singles title since [[Rafael Nadal]] in [[2019 Rogers Cup – Men's singles|2019]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/shelton-nakashima-montreal-2026-thursday-final|title=Shelton wins Montreal, joins Murray, Djokovic & Nadal in Canada history|website=ATPTour|date=14 August 2026}}</ref>

The fourth-round loss of [[Cameron Norrie]] guaranteed a first-time Masters 1000 finalist from the bottom half of the draw.<ref name=Canada2026QF>{{cite web|url= https://www.atptour.com/-/media/8db30bf862fd48f48babffdf98788d86.pdf|website=ATPTour|title=Montreal Notes Day 9|date=10 August 2026}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.atptour.com/en/news/jodar-lehecka-montreal-2026-saturday|title=Jodar sinks Lehecka in Montreal, Fils survives Norrie's late charge|website=ATPTour|date=12 August 2026}}</ref> Nakashima emerged to become that player.

This marked the first ATP Tour tournament in which all eight quarterfinalists were born in the 2000s, and the first Masters event since the [[2007 BNP Paribas Masters – Singles|2007 Paris Masters]] in which all eight quarterfinalists were aged 25 or under.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/montreal-2026-qf-history|title=What history has been made ahead of Montreal QFs?|website=atptour.com|publisher=ATP Tour|date=10 August 2026}}</ref> It also marked the first all-American Masters 1000 final since the [[2003 Western & Southern Financial Group Masters – Singles|2003 Cincinnati Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/jodar-nakashima-montreal-2026-wednesday|title=Nakashima ends Jodar's run in Montreal, reaches first Masters 1000 final|website=atptour.com|publisher=ATP Tour|date=13 August 2026}}</ref>
