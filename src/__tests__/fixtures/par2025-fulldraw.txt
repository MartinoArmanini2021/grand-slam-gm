[[Jannik Sinner]] defeated [[Félix Auger-Aliassime]] in the final, 6–4, 7–6<sup>(7–4)</sup> to win the singles tennis title at the [[2025 Rolex Paris Masters|2025 Paris Masters]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/tennis/jannik-sinner-paris-masters-result-alcaraz-no-1-b2856978.html|title=Jannik Sinner reclaims World No 1 from Carlos Alcaraz with Paris Masters title|publisher=The Independent|accessdate=2 November 2025}}</ref> It was his fifth [[ATP Masters 1000 tournaments|ATP Masters 1000]] title and 23rd [[ATP Tour]] title overall. Sinner did not lose a set en route to the title, and dropped only 29 games, the fewest since the event switched to [[hardcourt]]s in 2007.<ref name=number>{{cite web|title=Sinner wins maiden Paris title, reclaims World No. 1|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/sinner-auger-aliassime-paris-2025-final|publisher=ATPTour|website=atptour.com|date=2 November 2025|accessdate=2 November 2025}}</ref><ref>{{Cite web |date=2025-11-02 |title=Paris Masters: Jannik Sinner beats Felix Auger-Aliassime to win title and reclaim world number one ranking |url=https://www.bbc.co.uk/sport/tennis/articles/c14pvlde4ggo |access-date=2025-11-03 |website=BBC Sport |language=en-GB}}</ref> Sinner regained the [[List of ATP number 1 ranked singles tennis players|world No. 1 singles ranking]] by winning the title;<ref name=number/> [[Carlos Alcaraz]] was also in contention at the beginning of the tournament. 

[[Alexander Zverev]] was the defending champion,<ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/zverev-humbert-paris-2024-sunday|title=Relentless Zverev cruises past Humbert to Paris crown|date=2024-11-03|website=atptour.com |publisher=ATP|access-date=2024-11-03}}</ref> but lost in the semifinals to Sinner.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://tennisuptodate.com/atp/jannik-sinner-outclasses-alexander-zverev-in-paris-to-move-within-one-win-of-reclaiming-no-1|title=Jannik Sinner outclasses Alexander Zverev in Paris to move within one win of reclaiming No. 1|publisher=tennisuptodate.com|accessdate=1 November 2025}}</ref>
