[[File:P20250907DT-0489 President Donald Trump attends the U.S. Open Men’s Championship.jpg|thumb|Men's singles trophy]]
[[Carlos Alcaraz]] defeated defending champion [[Jannik Sinner]]<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2025-09-07/carlos_alcaraz_defeats_jannik_sinner_to_win_2025_us_open_mens_singles_title.html |title=Alcaraz defeats Sinner to win 2025 US Open mens singles title |date=September 7, 2025 |website=US Open}}</ref> in the final, 6–2, 3–6, 6–1, 6–4 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2025 US Open (tennis)|2025 US Open]].<ref>{{cite news |last=Braidwood |first=Jamie |date=7 September 2025 |url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/tennis/carlos-alcaraz-jannik-sinner-us-open-final-result-b2821925.html |title=Carlos Alcaraz dethrones Jannik Sinner to underline undeniable truth in US Open final |work=The Independent |accessdate=8 September 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |last=Jurejko |first=Jonathan |url= https://www.bbc.com/sport/tennis/articles/c8xrpd5jeveo|title=Alcaraz edges Sinner trilogy to win US Open |date=7 September 2025 |work=BBC Sport |accessdate=8 September 2025}}</ref>
It was his second [[US Open (tennis)|US Open]] title and sixth [[Grand Slam (tennis)#Tournaments|major]] title overall. By winning the title, Alcaraz regained the [[List of ATP number 1 ranked singles tennis players|world No. 1]] singles ranking from Sinner.<ref>{{cite news |last=Tomás Rico |first=Andrés |date=21 August 2025 |title=Analysis of the men's draw US Open 2025: Alcaraz coincides with Djokovic and Shelton; Sinner, with Draper and Zverev |url=https://www.puntodebreak.com/en/2025/08/21/analysis-of-the-mens-draw-us-open-2025-alcaraz-coincides-with-djokovic-and-shelton-sinner-with |work=Punto de Break |access-date=22 August 2025}}</ref> Alcaraz was the youngest man to win a sixth major title since [[Björn Borg]].<ref>{{cite news |last=Oddo |first=Chris |title= Carlos Alcaraz defeats Jannik Sinner for second US Open men's singles title |url= https://www.usopen.org/en_US/news/articles/2025-09-07/carlos_alcaraz_defeats_jannik_sinner_to_win_2025_us_open_mens_singles_title.html |date=7 September 2025 |access-date=9 September 2025|work=US Open}}</ref> Alcaraz also became the youngest man to win multiple slams on all 3 surfaces, at just {{ayd|2003|5|5|2025|9|7}} old, shattering [[Mats Wilander]]’s previous record of {{ayd|1964|8|22|1988|9|11}} old.  Sinner was the fourth (and youngest) man in the [[History of tennis#Open Era|Open Era]] to reach all four major finals in a season (after [[Rod Laver]], [[Roger Federer]], and [[Novak Djokovic]]), and the youngest in the Open Era to reach five consecutive major finals. [[Alcaraz–Sinner rivalry|Alcaraz and Sinner]] became the first pair of men's singles players in the Open Era to contest three major finals in the same year.<ref>{{cite news |title=Sinner sets winner-takes-all Alcaraz US Open final|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/sinner-auger-aliassime-us-open-2025-sf |date=6 September 2025 |access-date=9 September 2025 |work=ATP Tour}}</ref> For the first time since the introduction of the current [[ATP rankings]] in 1973, the world No. 1 and No. 2 contested all four 
major finals in a season.<ref>{{Cite web |last=West |first=Andy |date=10 July 2026 |title=Jannik Sinner motors past Novak Djokovic, sets Alexander Zverev Wimbledon title clash {{!}} ATP Tour {{!}} Tennis |url=http://www.atptour.com/en/news/sinner-djokovic-wimbledon-2026-sf |url-status=live |archive-url=https://megalodon.jp/2026-0711-0514-41/https://www.atptour.com:443/en/news/sinner-djokovic-wimbledon-2026-sf |archive-date=2026-07-10 |access-date=2026-07-10 |website=ATP Tour |language=en}}</ref>

At {{ayd|1987|5|22|2025|9|2}} of age, Djokovic was the oldest man to reach all four major semifinals in a season.<ref>{{cite news |last=Davies |first=Callum |url= https://tennishead.net/novak-djokovic-has-just-achieved-a-feat-that-no-player-in-the-open-era-ever-has-before-by-reaching-us-open-semi-finals/|title= Novak Djokovic has just achieved a feat that no player in the Open Era ever has before by reaching US Open semi-finals |website=Tennis Head|date=3 September 2025 |access-date=9 September 2025}}</ref> With his third-round win, Djokovic recorded his 192nd career match win at [[hardcourt]] majors, surpassing Federer's record.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/novak-djokovic-breaks-roger-federer-record-most-career-wins-at-hard-court-grand-slams |title=Novak Djokovic breaks Roger Federer's record for most career wins at hard-court Grand Slams |date=29 August 2025 |work=Tennis.com |access-date=30 August 2025}}</ref> With his semifinal loss to Alcaraz, this marked only the third year since 2007 where Djokovic did not contest a major final (after [[2009 ATP World Tour|2009]] and [[2017 ATP Tour|2017]]), and the first year since 2002 that none of [[Big Three (tennis)|Big Three]] contested a major final.<ref>{{cite news |last=Ilic |first=Jovica |date=6 September 2025 |title=Novak Djokovic's effort closes a 22-year-old chapter of Big 3 dominance |url=https://www.tennisworldusa.org/tennis/news/Novak_Djokovic/159320/novak-djokovic-s-effort-closes-a-22yearold-chapter-of-big-3-dominance |work=Tennis World USA |access-date=9 September 2025}}</ref> This also marked the first time since 2010 that [[Grigor Dimitrov]] did not participate at a major, ending his streak of 58 consecutive major appearances.<ref>{{cite news |url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/dimitrov-us-open-2025-withdrawal |title=Dimitrov withdraws from US Open, ending remarkable 58-major streak |website=ATP Tour|date=12 August 2025 |accessdate=12 August 2025}}</ref>

[[Coleman Wong]] became the first player from Hong Kong to qualify for a men's singles major in the Open Era, and was the first to win a major singles match since [[Ip Koon Hung]] and [[Edwin Tsai]] at the [[1954 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|1954 Wimbledon Championships]]—when Hong Kong was a [[British Hong Kong|British colony]].<ref>{{cite news |last=Eichenholz |first=Andrew |title=Coleman Wong: 'He's here to take people's lunch if necessary' |url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/wong-us-open-2025-feature |website=ATP Tour |date=30 August 2025 |access-date=30 August 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite news |date=August 26, 2025 |title=Coleman Wong 1st man from Hong Kong to win major singles match |url=https://www.espn.com.au/tennis/story/_/id/46072253/coleman-wong-advances-us-open-becomes-first-man-hong-kong-win-open-era-grand-slam-singles-match |work=ESPN |access-date=August 26, 2025}}</ref> A record-equaling five qualifiers (Wong, [[Leandro Riedi]], [[Jan-Lennard Struff]], [[Ugo Blanchet]], and [[Jérôme Kym]]) reached the third round, tying the tournament's Open Era record along with [[1984 US Open – Men's singles|1984]] and [[2021 US Open – Men's singles|2021]].<ref>{{cite news |last=Tingue |first=Jess |title= Meet the men's qualifiers in Round 3 of the 2025 US Open 2025}}</ref><ref>{{cite web |url=https://www.atptour.com/-/media/a8ea49240fa442d5b3daf669a6c4cd72.pdf |title=2025 US Open Day 6 Media Notes |date=29 August 2025 |website=ATP Tour}}</ref> Ranked No. 435, Riedi was the lowest-ranked player to reach the fourth round of a major since [[Richard Krajicek]] at the [[2002 Wimbledon Championships – Men's singles|2002 Wimbledon Championships]].<ref>{{cite
