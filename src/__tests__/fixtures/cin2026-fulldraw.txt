{{further|2026 Cincinnati Open}}

[[Arthur Fils]] defeated [[Frances Tiafoe]] in the final, 6–3, 1–6, 6–0 to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2026 Cincinnati Open]]. It was his first [[ATP Masters 1000 tournaments|ATP Masters 1000]] title, and fifth career [[ATP Tour]] title overall. Fils was the first Frenchman to win the [[Cincinnati Open]] title since [[Guy Forget]] in [[1991 Thriftway ATP Championships – Singles|1991]], and the first to win a Masters title overall since [[Jo-Wilfried Tsonga]] at the [[2014 Rogers Cup – Men's singles|2014 Canadian Open]].<ref>{{citeweb|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/fils-tiafoe-cincinnati-2026-final|title=Fils tops Tiafoe in Cincinnati to claim first Masters 1000 title|date=August 23, 2026|website=atptour.com}}</ref>

[[Carlos Alcaraz]] was the reigning champion,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2025/aug/18/carlos-alcaraz-wins-cincinnati-open-as-sinner-retires-from-final-with-illness|title=Carlos Alcaraz wins Cincinnati Open as Sinner retires from final with illness|publisher=The Guardian|accessdate=18 August 2025}}</ref> but did not participate due to a wrist injury.<ref name =Carlos2026>{{citeweb|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/alcaraz-cincinnati-2026-withdrawal|title=Alcaraz withdraws from Cincinnati|date=August 4, 2026|website=atptour.com}}</ref>

The loss of [[Taylor Fritz]] in the quarterfinals guaranteed a first-time Masters champion.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/fritz-nakashima-cincinnati-2026-friday-qfs|title=Nakashima continues hard-court surge, outlasts Fritz in Cincinnati QFs|publisher=atptour.com|date=21 August 2026}}</ref>
