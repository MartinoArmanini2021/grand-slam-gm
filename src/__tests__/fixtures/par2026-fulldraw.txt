[[Jannik Sinner]] was the reigning champion,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.independent.co.uk/sport/tennis/jannik-sinner-paris-masters-result-alcaraz-no-1-b2856978.html|title=Jannik Sinner reclaims World No 1 from Carlos Alcaraz with Paris Masters title|publisher=The Independent|accessdate=2 November 2025}}</ref> but withdrew before the tournament began due to a knee injury.<ref>{{citeweb|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/sinner-2026-injury-announcement-october|title=Sinner to miss rest of 2026 due to injury|website=atptour.com|date=6 October 2026}}</ref> Also, defending finalist [[Félix Auger-Aliassime]] withdrew due to a tweek in his ankle.<ref>{{Cite web |date=2026-10-18 |title=Felix Auger-Aliassime releases statement after withdrawing from two tournaments due to injury |url=https://tennishead.net/felix-auger-aliassime-releases-statement-after-withdrawing-from-two-tournaments-due-to-injury/ |access-date=2026-11-01 |website=tennishead.net |language=en-GB}}</ref>
