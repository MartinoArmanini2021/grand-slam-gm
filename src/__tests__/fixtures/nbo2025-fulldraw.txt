[[Ben Shelton]] defeated [[Karen Khachanov]] in the final, 6–7<sup>(5–7)</sup>, 6–4, 7–6<sup>(7–3)</sup> to win the men's singles tennis title at the [[2025 National Bank Open|2025 Canadian Open]]. It was his first [[ATP Masters 1000 tournaments|ATP 1000]] title and third career [[ATP Tour]] title.<ref>{{cite web|url= https://www.atptour.com/en/news/shelton-khachanov-toronto-final-2025 |title= Shelton claims ATP Masters 1000 breakthrough in Toronto! |website=ATPTour|date=8 August 2025 }}</ref> Shelton was the youngest American man to win a Masters title since [[Andy Roddick]] at the [[2004 NASDAQ-100 Open – Men's singles|2004 Miami Open]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/2025/aug/07/ben-shelton-toronto-open-title-khachanov|title=Ben Shelton rallies to beat Karen Khachanov in Toronto for biggest title of career|publisher=The Guardian|accessdate=8 August 2025}}</ref>

[[Alexei Popyrin]] was the defending champion,<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.theguardian.com/sport/article/2024/aug/13/alexei-popyrin-vs-andrey-rublev-montreal-masters-tennis-results|title=Alexei Popyrin stuns Andrey Rublev in Montreal Masters to end 21-year drought|website=[[TheGuardian.com]] |date=12 August 2024|accessdate=12 August 2024}}</ref> but lost in the quarterfinals to [[Alexander Zverev]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.abc.net.au/news/2025-08-05/alex-zverev-defeats-alexei-popyrin-at-canadian-open-in-tennis/105613896|title=Alexei Popyrin's Canadian Open defence ends in quarterfinal loss to Alexander Zverev|publisher=Australian Broadcasting Corporation|accessdate=5 August 2025}}</ref>

By reaching his first ATP 1000 final since the [[2018 Rolex Paris Masters – Singles|2018 Paris Masters]], Khachanov recorded the longest gap between successive ATP 1000 finals appearances, at six years and nine months.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/en/news/zverev-khachanov-toronto-2025-wednesday|title= Khachanov saves match point vs. Zverev to reach Toronto final|website=ATPTour|date=7 August 2025 }}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.atptour.com/-/media/e98877779dc74313a8b01074c13989c6.pdf|title= NATIONAL BANK OPEN - ATP MEDIA NOTES |publisher=ATPTour |date=7 August 2025}}</ref> With his third-round win, Zverev became the first man born in the 1990s to win 500 [[ATP Tour]] level main-draw singles matches, and the fifth active player to do so.<ref>{{Cite web |date=1 August 2025 |title= Zverev rolls into history: From Paris pain to 500 career wins |url= https://www.atptour.com/en/news/zverev-500-wins-feature-2025 |website=atptour}}</ref> [[Taylor Fritz]] became the first American man to reach the quarterfinals at all nine ATP 1000 tournaments.<ref>{{Cite web |date=4 August 2025 |title= Tie-break marathon: Fritz edges Lehecka without a single break |url= https://www.atptour.com/en/news/fritz-lehecka-toronto-2025-sunday|website=atptour}}</ref><ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/taylor-fritz-completes-masters-1000-quarterfinal-set-in-canada-first-american-lehecka|title=Taylor Fritz has now reached the quarterfinals or better at all nine Masters 1000 event|date=4 August 2025|website=Tennis}}</ref>

This event marked the final professional appearance of former Davis Cup and [[2014 Wimbledon Championships – Men's doubles|Wimbledon doubles]] champion, [[Vasek Pospisil]].<ref>{{cite news|url=https://apnews.com/article/vasek-pospisil-retires-wimbledon-ptpa-e5726cbe60cd85259a22483d9f8316ca|title=Vasek Pospisil retiring from tennis after a career that included a Wimbledon doubles title|date=21 July 2025|publisher=[[AP News]]}}</ref> He lost in the first round to [[Facundo Bagnis]].<ref>{{cite web|url=https://nationalbankopen.com/news/read/2025/sunday-1-nbo-toronto-results|title= Pospisil's Career Ends with Hard-Fought Loss; Opelka, Arseneault Among Sunday Winners|date=27 July 2025}}</ref>

== Seeded players ==
The following are the seeded players. Seedings are based on ATP rankings as of 21 July 2025. Rankings and points before are as of 28 July 2025.

Unlike the WTA, the ATP will only drop points from the week of 29 July 2024 ([[2024 Mubadala Citi DC Open – Men's singles|Washington]]) during this year's National Bank Open and backdate the ranking points at the end of the tournament to 4 August 2025. Points from the [[2024 National Bank Open]] held in Montreal will be dropped at the end of the [[2025 Cincinnati Open]] instead.<ref>{{cite web|url=https://www.tennis.com/news/articles/how-and-when-the-atp-and-wta-rankings-will-be-updated-for-canada-and-cincinnati|title=How and when will the ATP and WTA rankings be updated for Canada and Cincinnati?|date=August 6, 2025|website=tennis.com}}.</ref> Accordingly, the Points Defending column in the table below reflects the 19th best result (in parentheses) of players who are not defending points from Washington.
